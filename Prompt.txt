Goal: Split a set of teams into groups labeled with playing-card suits, using a fixed rule for how many groups to create and randomly assigning teams by “picking a card.”
Rules for number of groups (G) based on total teams (T):

Min groups = 1, Max groups = 4
Prefer even group counts (2 or 4)
Use this rule exactly:

If T ≤ 6, then G = 1
If 7 ≤ T ≤ 12, then G = 2
If T ≥ 13, then G = 4 (cap at 4 for any T, including >16)



Group labels (based on G):

G = 1: ♥ Hearts
G = 2: ♥ Hearts, ♦ Diamonds
G = 4: ♥ Hearts, ♦ Diamonds, ♣ Clubs, ♠ Spades

Random assignment method (“picking a card”):

Randomly shuffle the teams.
Deal to groups in round‑robin order using the suits above (use only the first G suits).
This ensures balanced sizes. If uneven (e.g., odd teams), earlier suits get one extra team in order: ♥, ♦, ♣, ♠.

Output requirements:

Show: Total teams (T), Groups used (G), Distribution by group (counts), and the Roster per group.
Maintain the suit order: ♥, ♦, ♣, ♠.
If team names are not provided, use Team 1, Team 2, … Team T.
The allocation must be random each time (i.e., not deterministic ordering).

Edge cases:

Odd team counts: allowed; distribute as evenly as possible (differences of at most 1).
T > 16: still use G = 4 and distribute evenly.

Examples for validation:

T = 6 → G = 1 (all in ♥)
T = 8 → G = 2 (♥ & ♦)
T = 12 → G = 2 (♥ & ♦)
T = 14 → G = 4 (♥, ♦, ♣, ♠)
T = 16 → G = 4 (♥, ♦, ♣, ♠)
